Our Heritage & Manifesto

"Built for people who still listen."

Founded in Mayfair, London in 2012, Vinyl emerged as a protest against digital music devaluation — establishing an international institution dedicated to master analog pressings and high-fidelity acoustics.

The Beginning

From Private Collection to Global Connoisseur Boutique

Vinyl began when founder Arthur Pendelton, a former Blue Note audio engineer and avid jazz collector, realized that rare first pressings were vanishing into private vaults without being properly documented or heard.

He established a private salon on Mount Street in London, bringing together ultrasonic cleaning technology, museum-grade sleeve preservation, and restored vintage Garrard turntables. What started as a private listening circle quickly grew into a worldwide sanctuary for serious vinyl collectors, record producers, and audiophiles.

In 2019, we expanded to our flagship boutique in Le Marais, Paris, featuring custom acoustically tuned listening chambers and dedicated estate cataloguing services.

Core Principles

The Five Pillars of Vinyl

Every record we acquire and every component we restore is governed by unyielding standards.

01

Uncompromising Authenticity

We never guess matrix numbers or pressing origins. Every LP is verified against master press logs, matrix etchings, label typography, and paper stock thickness.

02

Ultrasonic Curation

We believe physical surface dirt should never obscure musical beauty. All discs undergo multi-stage ultrasonic de-gunking before entering our collection catalog.

03

Analog Signal Integrity

We preserve pure continuous analog audio paths. No digital processing, no lossy conversion, only physical diamond tracing real vinyl grooves.

04

Estate Archival Respect

When cataloguing private estates, we honor the collector's life work by matching historical records with collectors who will cherish them for decades.

05

Communal Experience

A record store should be a living cultural hub. Our lounges invite deep conversation, tea tasting, and communal discovery across generations.

Strict Transparency

Our Curatorial Grading System

We strictly adhere to conservative Goldmine grading with zero inflation.

Mint (M) / Sealed

Flawless Original Condition

Factory unplayed condition, often still in original shrink wrap or perforated seal.

Near Mint (NM)

Virtually Imperceptible Wear

Disc shows high gloss sheen with zero surface scuffs or audible surface noise.

Excellent (EX / VG+)

Light Handling Marks

Minor cosmetic sleeve rub marks that do not affect pure playback fidelity.

Archival Vault (AV)

Museum Historical Relic

Ultra-rare 1st pressings with historical significance sold with complete provenance.
